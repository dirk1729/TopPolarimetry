Polarimeter Attention Net

How the top-polarimetry regression model turns a reconstructed probe jet, its tracks, and the surrounding event into a predicted cos θ — the polarimetric angle that carries the top quark's spin information.

+residual (skip) connection
Note on naming: “Stack Top” and “Stack Q1/Q2” are a holdover from an earlier version of this model that regressed separate top-quark and down-quark four-vectors. In the current single-output model, both branches feed the same regression head — there's no longer a physically distinct “Top” vs. “Quark” output, just two differently-processed views of the jet embedding (one shallow, one deep) concatenated together.
